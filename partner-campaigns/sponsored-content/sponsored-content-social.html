Campaign Wrap-Up & Key Takeaways
Goal exceeded — 2.76M impressions delivered (121% of 2.28M goal) on 95% of budget

The campaign over-delivered on its primary KPI by 477K impressions while spending just $8,054 of the $8,500 budget. The addition of Ep 4 (Azzi Fudd / WNBA Draft) pushed the campaign well past goal. Blended CPM of $4.32 on paid impressions. This is a strong campaign outcome to reference for future TastyTrade renewals.

TikTok was the organic breakout — 77% organic, 228% of contracted impressions, $1.94 CPM

TikTok delivered 592K views on just $267 in spend — more than double its contracted impression target. Ep 4's TikTok Dark post alone hit 325K views (100% organic, 11.86% ER). Ep 2's TikTok was also fully organic at 73K views and 10.4% ER. TikTok should receive a larger allocation in future content plans.

Total engagements hit 417K (15.1% blended ER) across all platforms

Ep 3 drove the highest engagement (215K) powered by YouTube Shorts paid engagement. Ep 4 drove 44K engagements on near-zero spend. Instagram consistently delivered high ERs (3.5%–9.95%) and LinkedIn held steady at 4.54% ER across all 4 episodes on zero spend.

YouTube Shorts: highest reach but most expensive — 80% of contracted goal at 141% of budget

YouTube Shorts delivered 858K impressions (80.2% of contracted 1.07M) while spending $2,989 (140.6% of $2,125 budget). At $3.91 CPM, it's the most expensive platform. However, Ep 3's Shorts surged to 468K views and drove 184K paid engagements. For future campaigns, consider capping YT Shorts budget and reallocating surplus to TikTok or IG for better efficiency.

Ep 4 (Fudd) proves the value of extending campaigns with timely content

Added post-contract to capitalize on the WNBA Draft narrative, Ep 4 delivered 382K impressions on just $74 in spend — essentially free incremental reach. The TikTok Dark post strategy drove massive organic distribution (325K views). This model — adding a timely bonus episode to an existing series — should be a standard recommendation for future sponsored content packages.

Come Up — Optimization & Spend Strategy
Budget at 60% — spend outpacing impressions heading into Draft weekend

With $10,206 of $16,875 spent (60.5%), the campaign has delivered 3.24M of 8M impressions (40.5%). Budget is outpacing impressions — IG at 65.4% spent, FB at 64.8%, and X at 58.6%. Need ~4.8M more impressions to hit 8M goal — will require organic acceleration or budget reallocation to maximize remaining spend efficiency.

X paid: cheapest reach ($1.04 CPM) but near-zero engagement — 634 eng on 1.4M impr (0.04% ER)

X Ads Manager is delivering 1,413,414 paid impressions on $1,466 spend at the lowest CPM of any platform ($1.04). However, X is generating only 634 engagements — a 0.04% ER that drags down the campaign's blended engagement rate. With $1,034 remaining of $2,500 contracted (58.6% spent), additional X spend will add impressions but further dilute ER. Recommend: let X budget pace naturally — do not accelerate. Reallocate remaining budget priority to IG and YT Shorts where engagement returns are meaningfully higher.

YouTube Shorts delivering exceptional engagement — 26.7% blended ER across 4 stories

All 4 YT Shorts posts are showing 24–35% ERs (Google Ads engagement definition). Tate leads at 34.8% ER with 13.3K engagements. With $1,686 of $3,592 spent (46.9%), YT Shorts has the most remaining budget headroom and is delivering the campaign's strongest engagement signal. Continue current spend trajectory.

Mauigoa breaks 1M — first story to cross the million-impression mark

Mauigoa leads all stories in total reach (1.04M) — the first to cross 1M — thanks to X paid delivering 538K at $1.16 CPM plus Meta paid adding 374K IG+FB impressions. Mendoza leads in engagement (47K) on 862K reach. All 4 stories are now above 635K+ total impressions. Key insight: X delivers the cheapest impressions but minimal engagement. Methodology Notes

ImpressionsTotal = MS Organic + Platform Ads Paid. Instagram/Facebook paid from Meta Ads Manager. X paid from X Ads Manager. YouTube Shorts paid from Google Ads. Organic from Measure Studio. YouTube Shorts organic uses MS Views Total.
CPMSpend ÷ Paid Impressions × 1,000. YouTube CPMs from Google Ads. Social CPMs from Measure Studio paid metrics.
Engagement RateTotal Engagements ÷ Total Views or Impressions. Uses platform-specific definitions (MS "Total Engagements" field).
Organic %Organic Views ÷ Total Views. LinkedIn is 100% organic (no paid). Facebook varies by campaign.
Facebook CPMEstimated from Total Spend ÷ Total Paid Views × 1,000 (MS doesn't report FB "Impressions - Paid" directly).
