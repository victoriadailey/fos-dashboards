CPM Outliers & Observations
TikTok delivers the lowest social CPM at $2.06–$2.61.

TastyTrade's TikTok spend of $191 across Eps 1 and 3 generated 93K paid impressions at $2.06 CPM. Comcast's heavier TikTok investment ($5,676 across 4 pieces) holds at $2.61. TikTok is the clear efficiency play for reach across both campaigns.

Comcast YouTube Shorts (Google Ads) averaged $3.16 CPM — 24% cheaper than TastyTrade's $4.11.

Comcast's 4 YouTube Shorts campaigns ranged from $2.74 (Course Tour) to $3.47 (Food Tour), while TastyTrade's 3 campaigns ranged $3.78–$4.42. Higher volume (2.2M vs 605K impressions) and longer content format likely drove better rates for Comcast.

Instagram CPMs vary 34% across campaigns — $3.85 (Comcast) vs $5.17 (TastyTrade).

TastyTrade's Instagram CPM is the highest paid social placement. However, Instagram also drives the strongest engagement rates (4.56% for TastyTrade, 0.88% for Comcast). Ep 3's Instagram hit 9.85% ER — the premium is justified by engagement quality.

Facebook CPM (~$4.03) is competitive for Comcast's reach-heavy approach.

Comcast's Facebook spent $4,999 generating 1.24M views (99.9% paid). While ER is negligible (0.05%), this is a pure reach play at a competitive CPM. Not an engagement channel — but effective for impression delivery.

Cross-Campaign Insights
TastyTrade on track to hit 2.28M goal — Ep 3 paid activation closed the gap.

At 89.7% delivered with 90% of flight elapsed, TastyTrade went from behind pace (64.9% at 78% elapsed) to nearly on track after Ep 3 paid activation drove 391K new impressions. With $1,684 remaining and 4 days left, the campaign should clear its goal.

Comcast hit 7M impressions on 94% of budget — tight, efficient delivery.

Cross-platform spend (TikTok $2.61, YouTube $3.16, IG $3.85) delivered the full impression goal in a 7-day flight with just $1.4K in budget remaining. The 4-platform, 4-content-piece strategy hit the mark. Strong model for future short-flight, high-reach campaigns.

TastyTrade engagement rate (2.90%) is 13x higher than Comcast (0.22%).

TastyTrade's March Madness content resonated deeply — especially on Instagram (4.56% ER) and LinkedIn (4.83% ER). Ep 3's IG post hit 9.85% ER, the highest of any post in either campaign. This reflects content-market fit (March Madness underdog stories) and platform selection. Comcast optimized for reach, not engagement — both approaches valid.

LinkedIn is a zero-cost engagement engine across both campaigns.

TastyTrade's 2 LinkedIn posts delivered 84K impressions and 4,082 engagements at 4.83% ER — all organic. Comcast doesn't include LinkedIn, but the TastyTrade data reinforces that LinkedIn should be a default inclusion for any sponsored social package.

Lauren's Comcast content outperformed Bailey's on engagement.

Lauren averaged 0.66% ER vs Bailey's 0.23% across identical platform distributions. Lauren's DITL Instagram post hit 2.24% ER — the standout Comcast social post. Consider skewing creator allocation toward Lauren for engagement-sensitive campaigns.

Short-form vertical video drives the best organic lift across both campaigns.

TastyTrade TikTok: 122K organic out of 240K total (51%). Comcast TikTok: 92K organic out of 2.2M total (4%). YouTube Shorts organic grew to 149K (up from 29K last refresh) as Ep 3 YouTube drove 120K organic views. TikTok is where the algorithm amplifies branded content most aggressively.

Optimization Recommendations
Campaign on track to hit goal — Ep 3 paid activation closed the gap

Ep 3 went from 0 paid spend to $1,544, delivering 391K impressions in 5 days. The campaign is now at 89.7% of goal with 4 days left and $1,684 in budget remaining. At current blended CPM ($4.55), remaining budget can deliver ~370K more impressions — more than enough to exceed the 2.28M goal.

Key Takeaways & Recommendations
Goal hit on 65% of budget — a highly efficient campaign

Delivering 7.01M impressions on $22,604 of a $24,000 budget (94.2%) shows tight budget management. The 4-platform strategy (YouTube + Instagram + TikTok + Facebook) distributed spend evenly and hit goal in a compressed 7-day flight. This is a model for future high-reach, short-flight campaigns.

TikTok was the efficiency champion at $2.61 CPM

TikTok delivered 2.23M views (31.8% of total campaign) on $5,676 in spend — the most impressions per dollar. It also contributed 92K organic views (4.1% organic). For future campaigns optimizing for reach, TikTok should receive the largest budget allocation.

Lauren's DITL Instagram post was the engagement standout (2.24% ER)

While Comcast's campaign overall optimized for reach (0.22% blended ER), Lauren's DITL Instagram post broke through with 2.24% ER and 4,152 engagements. Behind-the-scenes, day-in-the-life content clearly resonates more than produced recaps. Consider leading with DITL formats in future campaigns.

Facebook delivered impressions but near-zero engagement (0.05% ER)

Facebook generated 1.24M views (17.7% of total) on ~$5,000 spend — solid for reach. But with just 562 engagements (0.05% ER), it's a pure impression play. If future campaigns prioritize engagement alongside reach, consider reallocating Facebook budget to Instagram or TikTok.
