CPM Outliers & Observations
TikTok delivers the lowest social CPM at $2.11–$2.61.

TastyTrade's TikTok spend of just $125 on Ep 1 generated 59K paid impressions at $2.11 CPM. Comcast's heavier TikTok investment ($5,676 across 4 pieces) still holds at $2.61 — 36% cheaper than Instagram. TikTok is the clear efficiency play for reach.

Comcast YouTube Shorts (Google Ads) averaged $3.16 CPM — 22% cheaper than TastyTrade.

Comcast's 4 YouTube Shorts campaigns ranged from $2.74 (Course Tour) to $3.47 (Food Tour), while TastyTrade's 2 campaigns ranged $3.78–$4.42. Higher volume (2.2M vs 347K impressions) likely drove better rates for Comcast.

Instagram CPMs vary 32% across campaigns — $3.85 (Comcast) vs $5.10 (TastyTrade).

TastyTrade's Instagram CPM is the highest paid social placement. However, Instagram also drives the strongest engagement rates (3.77% for TastyTrade, 0.88% for Comcast). The premium may be justified by engagement quality — worth monitoring as Ep 3 scales.

Facebook CPM (~$4.03) is competitive for Comcast's reach-heavy approach.

Comcast's Facebook spent $4,999 generating 1.24M views (99.9% paid). While ER is negligible (0.05%), this is a pure reach play at a competitive CPM. Not an engagement channel — but effective for impression delivery.

Cross-Campaign Insights
TastyTrade needs ~800K impressions in 8 days to hit goal.

At 64.9% delivered with 78% of flight elapsed, the campaign needs to accelerate. Ep 3 just posted (4/1) with no paid amplification yet. Activating IG paid ($5.10 CPM) and YouTube Shorts (Google Ads at ~$4 CPM) on Ep 3 content is the fastest path to closing the gap with $4.6K in budget remaining.

Comcast hit 7M impressions on just 65% of budget — $8.3K remaining.

Efficient cross-platform spend (TikTok $2.61, YouTube $3.16, IG $3.85) delivered the full impression goal in a 7-day flight. The 4-platform, 4-content-piece strategy worked. This is a model to reference for future short-flight, high-reach campaigns.

TastyTrade engagement rate (3.13%) is 14x higher than Comcast (0.22%).

TastyTrade's March Madness content resonated deeply — especially on TikTok (5.00% ER) and LinkedIn (4.72% ER). This reflects both content-market fit (March Madness underdog stories) and platform selection (LinkedIn + TikTok organic). Comcast optimized for reach, not engagement — both approaches valid for their respective goals.

LinkedIn is a zero-cost engagement engine across both campaigns.

TastyTrade's 3 LinkedIn posts delivered 104K impressions and 4,934 engagements at 4.72% ER — all organic. Comcast doesn't include LinkedIn, but the TastyTrade data reinforces that LinkedIn should be a default inclusion for any sponsored social package.

Lauren's Comcast content outperformed Bailey's on engagement.

Lauren averaged 0.66% ER vs Bailey's 0.23% across identical platform distributions. Lauren's DITL Instagram post hit 2.24% ER — the standout Comcast social post. Consider skewing creator allocation toward Lauren for engagement-sensitive campaigns.

Short-form vertical video drives the best organic lift across both campaigns.

TastyTrade TikTok: 100K organic out of 159K total (63%). Comcast TikTok: 92K organic out of 2.2M total (4%). YouTube Shorts organic remains lower but still additive. TikTok is where the algorithm amplifies branded content most aggressively.

Key Takeaways & Recommendations
Goal hit on 65% of budget — a highly efficient campaign

Delivering 7.01M impressions on $15,664 of a $24,000 budget shows strong cost efficiency. The 4-platform strategy (YouTube + Instagram + TikTok + Facebook) distributed spend evenly and hit goal in a compressed 7-day flight. This is a model for future high-reach, short-flight campaigns.

TikTok was the efficiency champion at $2.61 CPM

TikTok delivered 2.23M views (31.8% of total campaign) on $5,676 in spend — the most impressions per dollar. It also contributed 92K organic views (4.1% organic). For future campaigns optimizing for reach, TikTok should receive the largest budget allocation.

Lauren's DITL Instagram post was the engagement standout (2.24% ER)

While Comcast's campaign overall optimized for reach (0.22% blended ER), Lauren's DITL Instagram post broke through with 2.24% ER and 4,152 engagements. Behind-the-scenes, day-in-the-life content clearly resonates more than produced recaps. Consider leading with DITL formats in future campaigns.

Facebook delivered impressions but near-zero engagement (0.05% ER)

Facebook generated 1.24M views (17.7% of total) on ~$5,000 spend — solid for reach. But with just 562 engagements (0.05% ER), it's a pure impression play. If future campaigns prioritize engagement alongside reach, consider reallocating Facebook budget to Instagram or TikTok.
