CPM Outliers & Observations

In-feed YouTube is the clear efficiency champion at $0.61 avg CPM. E*TRADE Ep 3 (Leslie Osborne) hit $0.46 CPM — the lowest across all campaigns. In-feed consistently delivers 88-89% of YouTube impressions at a fraction of pre-roll cost. This is the #1 lever for catching up on impression pacing.

Pre-roll CPMs vary dramatically — $7.65 to $14.92. State Farm pre-roll is running at $7.65 (well below the $13+ seen on ADP and E*TRADE). This may reflect audience targeting differences or campaign optimization. ADP pre-roll at $13.31 and E*TRADE at $13.91 are 45-82% more expensive per thousand impressions.

TikTok delivers the lowest social CPM at $1.87 avg. ADP TikTok at $1.43 is the most efficient paid social placement across all campaigns. Worth noting E*TRADE doesn't appear to have TikTok paid spend — a potential opportunity given the platform's CPM efficiency.

Instagram CPMs range from $3.41 (ADP) to $5.25 (State Farm). State Farm Instagram is 54% more expensive than ADP per thousand views. E*TRADE sits in the middle at $4.39. Despite higher CPMs, Instagram consistently delivers the strongest engagement rates of any paid social platform.

E*TRADE YouTube cutdowns are the most efficient shorts at $2.72 avg CPM. The Ep 1 Trailer at $2.09 CPM is the single cheapest cutdown/shorts placement. ADP cutdowns at $3.80 and State Farm at $4.13 are 40-52% more expensive. This suggests E*TRADE's shorter-form YouTube content is well-optimized for cost efficiency.

This dashboard is built from two data sources per campaign: Measure Studio (organic + boosted social post performance) and Google Ads (YouTube paid ad impressions, spend, and views). Both exports need to be current for the dashboard to be accurate.

Export both files, then provide them to update the dashboard. File naming convention: [Campaign] - [Series Name] [Source] (date).csv
