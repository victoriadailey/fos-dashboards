CPM Outliers & Observations
In-feed YouTube is the clear efficiency champion at $0.63 avg CPM.

E*TRADE Ep 3 (Leslie Osborne) hit $0.46 CPM — the lowest across all campaigns. In-feed consistently delivers 88-89% of YouTube impressions at a fraction of pre-roll cost. This is the #1 lever for catching up on impression pacing.

Pre-roll CPMs vary dramatically — $7.31 to $14.91.

State Farm pre-roll ran at $7.31 (well below the $13+ seen on ADP and E*TRADE). This may reflect audience targeting differences or campaign optimization. ADP pre-roll at $12.39–$14.02 and E*TRADE at $13.86 are 45-90% more expensive per thousand impressions.

X paid delivers the lowest social CPM at $0.80 (State Farm).

State Farm's X campaigns generated 3.60M impressions at $0.80 CPM — the most efficient paid social placement across all campaigns. TikTok is the next most efficient at $1.86 avg (ADP $1.55, SF $2.02). Worth noting E*TRADE has neither X nor TikTok paid spend.

Instagram CPMs range from $3.24 (ADP) to $4.79 (State Farm).

State Farm Instagram was 48% more expensive than ADP per thousand views. E*TRADE sits in the middle at $4.40. Despite higher CPMs, Instagram consistently delivers the strongest engagement rates of any paid social platform.

E*TRADE YouTube cutdowns are the most efficient shorts at $2.72 avg CPM.

E*TRADE Shorts are the cheapest cutdown/shorts placement. ADP Shorts at $3.78 and State Farm at $4.21 are 39-55% more expensive. This suggests E*TRADE's shorter-form YouTube content is well-optimized for cost efficiency.

Cross-Campaign Insights
ADP is significantly behind on impression pacing.

At 41.7% delivered with 75.3% of flight elapsed, the campaign is projected to finish at ~55% of its 6.6M goal. Recommend accelerating spend on high-efficiency platforms (TikTok CPM ~$1.55, YouTube in-feed CPM ~$0.49).

State Farm exceeded the 11.98M impression goal — 12.70M delivered (106%).

X paid campaigns (3.60M impressions at $0.80 CPM) were the missing piece — now the most efficient paid social channel in the campaign. Combined with YouTube in-feed (6.41M), the campaign exceeded goal with budget to spare ($10.6K of $29.5K remaining).

LinkedIn is the organic engagement leader across all campaigns.

All LinkedIn posts across ADP (2 posts), State Farm (3 posts), and E*TRADE (16 posts) achieved 100% organic reach with 3-6% ER — the highest across any platform. Consider increasing LinkedIn posting frequency as a zero-cost impression strategy.

E*TRADE Portfolio Players is significantly behind on impression pacing.

At 21.9% delivered with 50.3% of flight elapsed, the campaign is projected to finish at ~43% of the 41.6M goal. With all 5 YouTube episode campaigns ended and $146K+ in budget remaining, the biggest opportunity is scaling in-feed YouTube (CPM ~$0.53) and Instagram paid ($4.40 CPM).

Facebook is underperforming on engagement across E*TRADE.

11 posts with 331K views but only 32 total engagements (0.01% ER). State Farm's dark post also shows 93.8K views with 0 engagement. Recommend reallocating Facebook spend to higher-performing platforms like Instagram and LinkedIn.

YouTube Shorts and TikTok drive the highest organic view volumes.

State Farm YouTube Shorts hit 23-100% organic, and ADP TikTok reached 33.8% organic on 262K views. Short-form vertical video is the strongest organic format in the sponsored social mix.
