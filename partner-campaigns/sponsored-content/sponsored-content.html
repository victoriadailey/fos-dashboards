CPM Outliers & Observations
In-feed YouTube is the clear efficiency champion at $0.52 avg CPM.

E*TRADE Ep 3 (Leslie Osborne) hit $0.46 CPM — the lowest across all campaigns. ADP Ep 1 (Kim Ng) at $0.49 is close behind. In-feed consistently delivers 88-97% of YouTube impressions at a fraction of pre-roll cost. This is the #1 lever for catching up on impression pacing.

X paid delivers the lowest social CPM — ADP at $0.42, E*TRADE at $0.93.

ADP X campaigns averaged $0.42 CPM across 888K paid impressions. E*TRADE X at $0.93 CPM across 1.23M paid impressions. X is the most cost-effective paid social channel for both campaigns.

YouTube Shorts CPM converging around $3.24 across both campaigns.

ADP at $3.49 and E*TRADE at $3.12 — nearly identical efficiency. Both campaigns are seeing strong engagement on Shorts (ADP cutdowns: 99.3K eng, E*TRADE: included in 466.7K total).

ADP TikTok CPM at $2.09 — competitive with YouTube Shorts.

4 TikTok posts with $935 spend across 447K paid impressions. CPM of $2.09 (paid impressions only) is competitive with YouTube Shorts ($3.49). Ep 1 (Kim Ng) TikTok drove 264K total impressions with strong organic amplification (89K organic views).

Pre-roll CPMs remain elevated at $14+ across both campaigns.

ADP pre-roll at $14.02–$16.14 and E*TRADE at $13.86 avg. Pre-roll delivers guaranteed completion but at 25-30x the cost of in-feed. Reserve for brand awareness goals, not impression efficiency.

Cross-Campaign Insights
ADP is 15% behind on pacing — but gap is closing rapidly.

At 74.3% delivered with 89.3% of flight elapsed (26 days left). TikTok boosted data + X Ads narrowed the gap from 31% to 15%. With $4.3K remaining and in-feed CPM ~$0.51, the budget could theoretically deliver 8M+ more impressions — enough to close and exceed the 6.6M goal.

E*TRADE is 39.5% behind on pacing — significant gap remains.

At 30.6% delivered with 70.1% of flight elapsed (56 days left). All 7 YT episode campaigns ended. 31.2M impressions still needed with $27.2K budget remaining. The Ep 8 (Mark Cuban) teaser is generating 371K organic buzz — fast-tracking its YouTube launch is the highest-leverage move.

LinkedIn is the organic engagement leader across both campaigns.

ADP: 9 LI posts, 147K impressions, 5.7K eng (3.9–4.7% ER). E*TRADE: 23 LI posts, 398K impressions, 16.7K eng (4.2% ER). Every LI post is 100% organic. Increasing posting frequency is the highest-ROI zero-cost tactic available.

X Ads now integrated across both campaigns — highest-efficiency paid social.

ADP X: $0.42 CPM (888K paid impr). E*TRADE X: $0.93 CPM (1.23M paid impr). Combined: 2.1M paid X impressions at $0.71 avg CPM. X is the most efficient paid social channel — ~12x more efficient than Instagram on a CPM basis.

Combined YT Paid Engagements across both campaigns: 649K.

ADP: 182.7K (83K full ep + 100K cutdowns). E*TRADE: 466.7K across 7 episodes. YouTube engagement depth is the standout metric — vastly exceeding social engagement (ADP 14.8K, E*TRADE 62.8K).

Instagram drives the highest social engagement rate across both campaigns.

ADP IG: 2.11% ER at $4.87 CPM. E*TRADE IG: 1.53% ER at $5.25 CPM. Instagram is the engagement quality play — higher CPM reflects boosted delivery, but engagement depth justifies the investment for brand resonance.
